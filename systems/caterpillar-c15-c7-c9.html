Technical FAQ

Questions rebuilders ask about the CAT C15 / C7 / C9

What is the difference between the Cat C7 / C9 and C15 injectors?

The C7 and C9 use HEUI injectors, which are actuated by high pressure engine oil and controlled by a solenoid. The C15 uses MEUI, a unit injector driven mechanically from the camshaft with electronic control of fuel delivery. The parts and the tooling are different for each family.

Why does oil-to-fuel seal protection matter on a HEUI injector?

A HEUI injector sits between engine oil and diesel fuel. If the seals fail or are damaged during a rebuild, the two fluids mix. The Diesel Tech Academy C7/C9 course covers seal protection and measuring needle lift by subtraction.

Which Robiel tool sets do you list for Caterpillar injectors?

Robiel 323.50.261 covers the Cat C7 / C9 and Navistar unit injectors and 323.50.42 the C7 / C9 pump. For the C15, 323.50.39 covers Delphi E3 heavy duty / Cat 15 units and 323.50.221 covers Delphi EUI for Cat C10, C12, C15 and C18.
