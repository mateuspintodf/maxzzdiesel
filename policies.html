Our policies

Clear terms for wholesale accounts.

These policies apply to orders placed with Maxzz Diesel by quote or through the B2B portal. Your account manager can confirm the terms for your account.

  • Most orders ship the same business day
  • Core credit on reman parts
  • Manufacturer warranty on genuine parts
  • Returns by authorization
Shipping

Orders ship from our warehouse in Coconut Creek, Florida. Most orders confirmed before the daily cutoff ship the same business day.

We ship across North America and Latin America. Freight terms, carriers and export documents are confirmed on your quote.

Core returns

Remanufactured injectors and pumps carry a core charge, shown on your quote.

Return the used unit in rebuildable condition, in the box the reman part came in. Once we inspect it, the core charge is credited back to your account.

Returns

Contact our team before sending any part back. We issue a return authorization number that must appear on the box.

Parts must be new, unused and in the original packaging. Electrical parts and special orders may not be returnable.

Warranty

Genuine parts carry the warranty of their manufacturer. Remanufactured parts carry the warranty of their remanufacturer.

To open a claim, send the order number, the part number and a short description of the failure to sales@maxzzdiesel.com.
