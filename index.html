Who we are

Diesel specialists for over 30 years.

Maxzz Diesel began as TW Diesel in São Paulo, Brazil, in 1992. Founder Waldyr Mazzer started out as an instructor for Bosch and Delphi. His sons, Douglas and Michael, joined the business and brought it to the US in 2013.

  • Founded in São Paulo in 1992
  • Started by a Bosch and Delphi instructor
  • In the US since 2013

Diesel injection is all we do. We supply complete injectors, pumps and rails, the components to rebuild them, and the kits, tools and fluids that finish the job.

  • Injectors, pumps and rails
  • Repair parts from Bosch, LEAD and Fratelli Bosio
  • Kits, bench tools and fluids

From South Florida we serve rebuilders, injection labs and distributors across the Americas, Europe, Africa, Asia and the Middle East.

  • US stock in South Florida
  • Proud member of the Association of Diesel Specialists
  • Customers on five continents
